Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Video Automation ("the tool") is a self-hosted application operated by its owner for the owner's own channels. This policy explains what Google user data the tool accesses, how it is used and stored, and how to revoke access.

1. Data the tool accesses

When the channel owner connects a YouTube account, the tool requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos, with titles, descriptions and settings the owner has approved, to the connected channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read statistics (view, like and comment counts) of the owner's own uploaded videos.

The tool stores OAuth access and refresh tokens so it can act on the owner's behalf. It does not collect or store passwords.

2. How the data is used

  • Tokens are used only to upload the owner's approved videos and to read the owner's own video statistics.
  • Statistics are shown to the owner. Titles and view counts of the owner's own best-performing videos may be included in prompts sent to an AI service provider (Anthropic, the provider of the Claude model) so that future video scripts can follow what works for that channel. No personal information about viewers is accessed or sent.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to anyone else except as described above or as required by law.
  • The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and generated content are stored on the computer or server where the tool is installed, in files readable only by the operator. They are not uploaded to any third-party storage by the tool.

4. Retention and revoking access

  • The owner can disconnect a channel inside the tool at any time, which deletes the stored tokens.
  • Access can also be revoked from the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Videos already uploaded remain on YouTube until the owner deletes them there.

5. Children

The tool is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
