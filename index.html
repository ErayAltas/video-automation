Video Automation

A personal tool for creating and publishing short educational videos.

What it does

Video Automation is a self-hosted tool that helps a channel owner produce short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels). It writes scripts, adds narration, renders the video, prepares a title and description for each platform, and, after the owner approves, uploads the video to the owner's own YouTube channel.

How it uses your YouTube account

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to publish videos that the channel owner has reviewed and approved, to the channel the owner connected.
  • Read basic statistics (youtube.readonly): to read view and like counts of the owner's own videos, so the tool can show performance and tune future scripts.

The tool only acts on the channel the owner connects. It does not access other people's accounts, comments or private messages, and it does not publish anything the owner has not approved (unless the owner explicitly enables automatic approval).

Your data

Access tokens are stored only on the computer or server where the tool runs. They are not shared with anyone. See the Privacy Policy for details, including how to revoke access at any time.
